# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other
project space, and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and welcome people of every
background and experience level. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, publishing
someone's private information, and sexualized language or imagery are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Email [team@genex.games](mailto:team@genex.games) with the subject “Genex conduct report”.
Include links or a description of what happened and how we can contact you. Reports are handled
confidentially; information is shared only with people needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers review every report and act as the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
describe: a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, depending on the impact.
